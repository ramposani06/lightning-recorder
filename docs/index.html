Colour = the model's chance that a reported lightning-caused fire follows within 3 days in the 3x3 cells (about 33 km) around the square. "real" = how often that really happened in 21 years of tests.
What this number is NOT
It is not the chance that one strike starts a fire, not the chance of any wildfire, and not human-caused fires. About 1 in 4 lightning fires (more in the far north) have no ground lightning nearby beforehand, so this map cannot show them. Hover a square for its inputs.
